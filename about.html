About Baripas Studio

Independent mobile game studio in Türkiye · Last updated 6 November 2026

Who we are. Baripas Studio is an independent single-player mobile game studio based in Türkiye. We build calm, focused games for iPhone, iPad and Android. Our flagship title is Cube Pattern, a free 3D cube memory game. We also publish Swipe Clone and Angle Dare.

What we build

We make small games that respect the player: no feed, no chat, no account required, and no pay-to-win. Games are offline-first, with optional sign-in only for cloud sync, leaderboards and social features. Analytics and crash reporting are opt-in.

Our principles

  • Player first. The full game is playable for free; purchases are optional cosmetics and convenience.
  • No dark patterns. No forced ads, no loot boxes, no pay-to-win.
  • Privacy by default. Guest play works without an account; analytics and crash reports are opt-in.
  • Offline first. Story levels and modes work without internet.
  • Transparency. Account deletion is available in the app and on the web; support answers within 2 business days.

Cube Pattern

Cube Pattern is a free 3D spatial memory puzzle. Players watch a pattern of glowing tiles on a rotating cube, then rebuild the sequence across faces. It includes 100 story levels in 10 chapters, a daily challenge, four game modes and six languages. Read how to play, the changelog or the explainer on spatial memory.
