What makes it different

Most mobile memory games use a 2D grid. Cube Pattern uses a rotating 3D cube: players must remember which tile and which face, then update that spatial representation while rotating the cube. Later levels add decoys, hidden faces, reverse order, single-look memory and mirrored patterns.

Frequently asked questions

What is Cube Pattern?

Cube Pattern is a free 3D spatial memory game for iPhone, iPad and Android. Players memorize a pattern of glowing tiles on a rotating cube, then rebuild the sequence by tapping the tiles.

Who develops Cube Pattern?

Baripas Studio, an independent single-player mobile game studio based in Türkiye.

Is Cube Pattern free?

Yes. Download and play are free. Optional purchases include premium (monthly, yearly, lifetime), coin packs, hint packs, a starter pack and cosmetic collections. Rewarded ads are optional.

What platforms and languages are supported?

iPhone and iPad on iOS/iPadOS 16 or later, and Android phones and tablets on Android 8.0 or later. Languages: English, Turkish, German, Spanish, French and Portuguese.

Does Cube Pattern work offline?

Yes. Story levels and game modes work without an internet connection. Sign-in, cloud sync, leaderboards, friends, ghost challenges and live events need a connection.

How many levels and modes are there?

100 story levels in 10 chapters, a three-round Master Cube finale, a daily challenge (UTC), and four modes: Memory Climb, 60s Blitz, Zen and Daily Challenge.

Do I need an account?

No. Guest play is supported. Optional Google sign-in enables cloud sync, ranked daily scores, leaderboards, friends and ghost challenges. Account deletion is available in the app and on the web.

Is Cube Pattern a medical or brain-training treatment?

No. It is an entertainment game that practices a spatial memory task (pattern encoding, mental rotation and ordered recall). It makes no medical claims.
