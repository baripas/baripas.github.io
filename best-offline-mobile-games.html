Best offline mobile games for flights and travel (2026)

Editorial guide by Baripas Studio · Last updated 6 November 2026

Quick answer. For a flight, pick a single-player game that needs no connection: puzzle, memory, card/roguelike deckbuilder or word game. Our free memory pick is Cube Pattern — 100 levels, a daily challenge (playable offline), four modes and six languages. Download levels ahead; no internet needed for story or modes.

What to look for

  • Single-player and offline: no login required for the core game.
  • Short sessions: one to five minutes per round fits boarding, queues and short flights.
  • Low battery/data: avoid always-online games and video-heavy titles.
  • Pause-friendly: you can stop at any moment.

Before you fly

  • Open the game once at home so any assets are cached.
  • Download offline maps/entertainment separately.
  • Cube Pattern: sign-in is optional; guest mode works offline. Cloud sync catches up later.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good offline game for a flight?

A single-player puzzle or memory game with no login requirement. Cube Pattern works offline for story levels and modes and needs no account.

Does Cube Pattern work in airplane mode?

Yes for story levels and game modes; sign-in, sync, leaderboards and events need internet later.

Are offline games free?

Many are free with ads or purchases; some are premium. Cube Pattern is free with optional purchases.
