What is Cube Pattern?

Cube Pattern is a memory puzzle built around a rotating 3D cube. Instead of matching pairs, you memorize where tiles light up on the cube's faces — then reproduce the pattern in the correct order before the timer runs out. It is a calm, single-player brain game: no feed, no chat, no pressure beyond your own best score.

How to play

  1. Watch the pattern: tiles light up on the cube for a few seconds.
  2. Rotate the cube with one finger to inspect every face.
  3. Tap the tiles in the right order to rebuild the pattern.
  4. Earn up to three stars per level and keep your daily streak alive.

Game modes

  • Memory Climb — endless run: start with three tiles and add one after every success. Three mistakes end the run.
  • 60s Blitz — solve as many 2–5 tile micro-patterns as possible in one minute.
  • Zen — no timer, no lives, just the pattern.
  • Daily Challenge — a new pattern every day (UTC) with a fixed reward and a streak that survives one missed day.

Features

  • 100 story levels across 10 themed chapters, ending with the three-round Master Cube.
  • Optional boosters: Hint, Time Freeze, Replay and Shield.
  • A memory profile that tracks eight skill dimensions, plus achievements and a weekly leaderboard.
  • Cosmetics: background themes, cube palettes, face markings, mascots and ambient audio packs.
  • Live events with themed rewards, friend codes and ghost challenges.
  • Optional rewarded ads for extra coins — never required to play.

Premium and purchases

The game is free. Optional in-app purchases include a premium upgrade — no ads, every theme unlocked, unlimited lives, premium levels and triple daily-challenge rewards — plus cosmetic collections, a starter pack, hint packs and coin packs. Prices are localized by the App Store and Google Play, and the store listing is the authoritative source for the price in your region. Restoring purchases works from the paywall in the app.

Languages

Cube Pattern is fully localized in six languages: English, Turkish, German, Spanish, French and Portuguese. Daily challenge dates use UTC so every player gets the same pattern on the same day.

Frequently asked questions

Is Cube Pattern free?

Yes. It is free to download and play. Purchases are optional and rewarded ads are opt-in.

Can I play offline?

Yes. Story levels and game modes work offline. Sign-in, cloud sync, leaderboards, friends and live events need a connection.

Does it work on tablets?

Yes, Cube Pattern runs on iPad and Android tablets. The game itself is portrait-first.

Do I need an account?

No. You can play as a guest; signing in is only needed to sync progress, submit ranked daily scores and use social features.

How many levels are there?

100 levels in 10 chapters, plus the daily challenge and the four endless/score modes.

Is there a subscription?

There is an optional monthly and yearly premium subscription, plus a one-time lifetime unlock. All are optional.
