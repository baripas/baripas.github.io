Cube Pattern — frequently asked questions

Short, direct answers about the game, purchases, accounts and privacy · Last updated 6 November 2026

Cube Pattern in one minute. A free 3D cube memory game for iPhone, iPad and Android by Baripas Studio. Watch a pattern of glowing tiles on a rotating cube, then rebuild it. 100 levels in 10 chapters, a daily challenge, four game modes, six languages and offline play. Optional premium and cosmetic purchases; rewarded ads are optional.

Game basics

What is Cube Pattern?

Cube Pattern is a free 3D spatial memory puzzle for iPhone, iPad and Android. A pattern of glowing tiles appears on a rotating cube for a few seconds; you then rotate the cube and tap the tiles in the correct order to rebuild the pattern.

Who makes Cube Pattern?

Cube Pattern is made by Baripas Studio, an independent single-player mobile game studio based in Türkiye.

Is Cube Pattern free?

Yes. Download and play are free. Optional purchases include premium (monthly, yearly or lifetime), coin packs, hint packs, a starter pack and cosmetic collections. Rewarded ads are optional.

Does Cube Pattern work offline?

Yes. Story levels and all game modes work without an internet connection. Sign-in, cloud sync, leaderboards, friends, ghost challenges and live events need a connection.

What devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad on iOS/iPadOS 16.0 or later, and Android phones and tablets on Android 8.0 or later.

What languages does it support?

English, Turkish, German, Spanish, French and Portuguese.

How to play

How do I play Cube Pattern?

Watch the glowing tile pattern, drag with one finger to rotate the cube, then tap the tiles in the same order to rebuild the pattern. Accuracy and speed decide your stars.

How many levels are there?

100 story levels across 10 themed chapters, ending with a three-round Master Cube finale. There is also a daily challenge and four game modes.

How many stars can I get per level?

Up to three stars per level. Stars unlock levels, achievements and leaderboard progress.

What are the game modes?

Memory Climb (endless, one extra tile per success), 60s Blitz (as many 2–5 tile micro-patterns as possible in a minute), Zen (no timer or lives) and the Daily Challenge (one pattern per UTC day).

How do stars and coins work?

Stars measure your result per level. Coins are earned from level stars, the daily challenge, achievements, events and optional rewarded ads, and are spent on cosmetics and boosters.

What are boosters?

Hint, Time Freeze, Replay and Shield. They are optional helpers, earned through play and events or bought with coins.

Is there a daily challenge?

Yes. A new pattern appears every day at 00:00 UTC with a fixed reward and a streak that survives one missed day. Signed-in players can submit a ranked score.

How long is a session?

Most levels take one to three minutes; 60s Blitz is exactly one minute, and Zen has no timer. It is designed for short sessions.

Purchases and premium

What does premium include?

Premium removes ads, gives unlimited lives, unlocks all themes and triples daily-challenge rewards (100 → 300 coins). It is available as a monthly or yearly subscription and as a one-time lifetime purchase.

How do I restore purchases?

Open Settings → Restore Purchases, or use the Restore option on the Premium screen. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you used to buy.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google, not by the app. On iPhone: Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. On Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their policies. Request one in your App Store or Google Play purchase history; the studio cannot issue store refunds directly.

Why does the store say I already own an item?

Recent versions detect this and restore the item automatically. If it does not appear, tap Restore Purchases; if it still fails, contact contact@baripas.studio.

Are there ads?

Where ads are enabled, Unity LevelPlay serves rewarded and natural-break ads after your privacy choice. Ads are optional and premium removes them.

Account, sync and privacy

Do I need an account?

No. You can play as a guest. Google sign-in is optional and enables cloud sync, leaderboards, friends and ghost challenges.

How does cloud sync work?

Sign in with Google on each device to sync progress, coins and cosmetics. Guest progress stays on the device and is not synced.

What happens if I reinstall the app?

Guest progress is stored locally and is lost when the app is uninstalled. If you signed in with Google, your cloud progress can be restored after signing in again.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings → Delete Account (two-step confirmation). You can also use the web page /account-deletion/. Cloud data is deleted; store subscriptions must be cancelled separately in the store.

What data do you collect?

Progress and settings are stored locally; sales are processed by Apple/Google via RevenueCat. Analytics and crash reports are opt-in; rewarded ads use Unity LevelPlay after your privacy choice. See the privacy policy.

Is Cube Pattern suitable for children?

Cube Pattern is designed for a general audience: no violence, no chat and no user-generated content feeds. Optional purchases and ads exist; use device parental controls if needed. The store listing shows the official age rating.

Does Cube Pattern train memory or brain skills?

It is practice at a spatial memory task: pattern encoding, mental rotation and ordered recall. Research on 3D games suggests task-specific practice effects, but Cube Pattern is entertainment, not a medical or cognitive treatment.
