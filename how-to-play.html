How to play Cube Pattern

Rules, scoring and tips for the 3D cube memory game · Last updated 6 November 2026

Quick answer. Watch the glowing tile pattern on the cube, rotate the cube with one finger, then tap the tiles in the same order to rebuild the pattern. You earn up to three stars per level, plus coins from the daily challenge, achievements and events.

Step by step

  1. Watch the pattern. Tiles light up on the cube for a few seconds. Remember which tiles and which faces are used.
  2. Rotate the cube. Drag with one finger to inspect every face.
  3. Rebuild the sequence. Tap the tiles in the correct order. Accuracy and speed decide your stars.
  4. Keep the streak. Play the daily challenge every day (UTC) to keep your streak and earn coins.

Scoring, stars and coins

StarsUp to 3 per level; stars unlock levels, achievements and leaderboard progress
CoinsEarned from level stars, the daily challenge (100 base, 300 with premium), achievements, events and optional rewarded ads
SpendingCoins buy cosmetics and boosters in the shop; premium removes ads and unlocks themes and extra daily rewards

The four game modes

  • Memory Climb — endless: start with three tiles, add one after each success. Three mistakes end the run.
  • 60s Blitz — solve as many 2–5 tile micro-patterns as possible in one minute.
  • Zen — no timer and no lives, just the pattern.
  • Daily Challenge — one pattern per UTC day with a fixed reward and ranked score.

Boosters

Hint highlights a correct tile, Time Freeze pauses the timer, Replay shows the pattern again, and Shield absorbs one mistake. Boosters are optional and can be earned through play or events.

Tips

  • Memorize the shape of the pattern first, then the faces.
  • Rotate once, slowly: a stable view is easier to remember than many small drags.
  • Use the Zen mode to practice sequences without time pressure.
  • Play the daily challenge early: a streak survives one missed day.

FAQ

How many stars can I earn per level?

Up to three stars per level.

What are the four game modes?

Memory Climb, 60s Blitz, Zen and the Daily Challenge.

Can I play offline?

Yes, story levels and modes work offline; sync and leaderboards need a connection.

How do boosters work?

Hint, Time Freeze, Replay and Shield are optional and earned through play or bought in the shop.
