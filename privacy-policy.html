Privacy Policy

Effective 26 October 2026 · Applies to Angle Dare, Swipe Clone and other Baripas Studio mobile games

Baripas Studio (“we”) publishes small single-player mobile games. This policy explains what the games and their service providers process. The games work offline and do not require an account.

1. Data stored on your device

The games store progress locally: scores, completed rounds, Gold, Daily progress, challenge codes you create, equipped cosmetics, settings, language, tutorial completion and Premium status. This data stays on your device unless you use an operating-system backup. You can remove it by clearing the app data or uninstalling the game.

2. Gameplay input

  • Angle Dare reads the device's rotation/motion sensor while you play, to measure an angle. Processing happens on the device in real time. Raw sensor data is not recorded, uploaded or sold.
  • Swipe Clone holds your swipe path in memory while the round is scored. Raw gesture coordinates are not sent to analytics and are not kept after the round.

3. Purchases

Premium is sold through Apple's App Store or Google Play: a monthly auto-renewing subscription and a one-time, non-consumable lifetime unlock. The store processes payment. We do not receive or store card details, bank details, receipts or purchase tokens. Billing, renewal, cancellation, restoration and refunds are controlled by the store account.

4. Advertising

Where ads are enabled, Unity LevelPlay is the only mediation integration. The selected demand sources are Unity Ads and Liftoff (Vungle) adapters. Ads are requested only after the applicable privacy choice and eligibility checks pass. Premium disables ads. The current Swipe Clone build ships without production LevelPlay credentials, so no ad network is initialized there; a later release that enables ads will update this policy and the store declarations first.

5. Analytics

The current production code uses a no-op analytics sink. Raw swipe coordinates, challenge payloads, purchase tokens and recipient lists are never analytics fields. Adding an analytics vendor requires a policy and store-declaration update before activation.

6. Sharing

When you choose Share, the operating system share sheet handles the generated text or image. We do not store the recipient list or a copy of the shared content.

7. Support

If you email us or report an ad, we use the message, contact address, device details and attachments only to investigate and answer the request. Do not send payment credentials or unnecessary personal information.

8. Retention and deletion

Local game data remains on the device until you clear the app data or uninstall the game. Consent choices are stored in the same local persistence and can be changed in Settings → Manage privacy choices. Store records follow the store's retention rules. Support messages are kept only as long as needed to resolve the request and meet legal duties.

9. Your choices and rights

You can play without an account. In Settings → Manage privacy choices you can change advertising permission and the regional opt-out signal; a denied or unresolved choice prevents ad initialization. Depending on your location, you may request access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction or information about processing, or object to a processing activity. Because game state is local, most requests can be completed by clearing app data. For support messages or vendor questions, email contact@baripas.studio. You may also contact your local data protection authority.

10. Children

The games are for a general audience and are not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child sent personal information to support, contact us so it can be reviewed and deleted where required.

11. Service providers and transfers

The games use platform services for distribution and billing, and may use Unity LevelPlay and its selected adapters where ads are enabled. Those providers process data under their own notices and may transfer it internationally. No AdMob, AppLovin MAX, direct Vungle/Liftoff mediation stack or other mediation provider is part of these games.

12. Security and changes

The games use platform storage and transport and keep only the local data described above. No method is completely secure. We may update this policy when the games, SDKs, vendors or legal requirements change; the revised effective date will be published here.

13. Contact

Privacy questions, deletion requests and rights requests: contact@baripas.studio.
