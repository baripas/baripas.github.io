Swipe Clone

My score does not match what I drew

The score has three parts: shape (60%), pace (25%) and duration (15%). A close shape with a different tempo still loses points. The result screen names the weakest part and the game shows the reference line and pace dots while you trace. Tap the replay icon to watch the path again.

The demo line is too fast or too slow

Every round is generated from a seed and scaled to its path length. If a line feels wrong, replay the same round: the same seed always produces the same path and timing.

I stopped early and still passed

Stopping well before the end marker caps the score below the pass mark. If you see a pass with a short trace, email us with the round seed shown on the game screen.

Angle Dare

The angle does not move / sensor trouble

Angle Dare needs a device with a rotation-vector motion sensor. Turn the phone slowly and hold it upright; very old or heavily modified devices may not report the sensor. Close and reopen the app, then tap Retry. The simulator cannot play the real sensor loop.

Purchases and restore

Premium is available as a monthly subscription and a one-time lifetime unlock. Restore Purchases is on the Premium screen and in Settings. Monthly Premium renews through Apple or Google; cancel or manage it in the store account settings. Lifetime does not renew. Refunds are handled by the store.

Ads and privacy

Where ads are enabled, Unity LevelPlay serves rewarded and natural-break ads after your privacy choice. Premium removes ads. Manage the advertising choice in Settings → Manage privacy choices. For an ad that was inappropriate or broken, use Report an ad in Settings or email us with the game, time and placement.

Deleting your data

Game progress is local. Clear the app's data or uninstall the game to remove it. Store purchase records remain with Apple or Google. Support emails are deleted when the request is resolved unless we must keep them for legal reasons.
