Cube Pattern — troubleshooting

Fixes for purchases, premium, sync, sign-in, progress, notifications and crashes · Last updated 6 November 2026

First three steps for almost every issue: 1) update the app, 2) restart it, 3) open Settings → Restore Purchases and check the sync line. Still stuck? Email contact@baripas.studio with your app version, device model and a screenshot.

1. A purchase completed but the item, coins or premium is missing

Wait 10–30 seconds and reopen the app; store confirmation can arrive after the purchase sheet closes. Then open Settings → Restore Purchases. If you bought premium, check Settings → Premium status. Coins and non-consumable items are delivered by the server after store verification; a weak connection can delay it. Still missing after 10 minutes? Email contact@baripas.studio with your store receipt (order ID) and app version.

2. The store says “you already own this item”

This means your Apple or Google account already bought the item. You do not need to buy it again. Update to the latest app version (recent versions restore it automatically) or tap Restore Purchases. If the item still does not appear, contact support with a screenshot of the error and your account email.

3. Premium is active but the app still shows a paywall

Sign out and back in, then open Settings → Restore Purchases. Premium is tied to the Apple ID or Google account that purchased it; if you switched store accounts, restore with the original one. Monthly/yearly premium renews through the store; lifetime is a one-time unlock. If the store shows an active subscription but the app does not, email contact@baripas.studio with a screenshot of the subscription and Settings → Premium status.

4. Coins are shown but I cannot spend them

Update to the latest version: older builds could show coins that were not yet written to the server wallet. Then reopen the shop. If the shop says it is in test mode, update the app; rollout restrictions were removed. If it still fails, send your app version and a screenshot.

5. Progress sync is stuck on “pending”

Update to the latest version (a profile-merge bug caused this in builds before 1.0.0 build 5 / vc67). Then sign out and sign in again with Google; open Settings and wait for “synced”. If it stays pending, email support with your app version and the Google account email (not the password).

6. Google sign-in does not work

Check that the device has internet and a browser/Google Play Services (Android) or Safari (iOS) available. Close the Google sheet and try again. If the browser shows a redirect error, update the app; if it persists, send a screenshot of the error to contact@baripas.studio.

7. My progress disappeared after reinstalling the app

Guest progress is stored only on the device and is removed when the app is uninstalled. Sign in with Google to sync progress to the cloud, then it can be restored on the same or another device. If you were signed in and progress is still missing, contact support with your account email and approximate level/star count.

8. A collection or theme does not appear after buying

Tap Restore Purchases in Settings. Collections are delivered to your app account by the server after store verification. If you bought it on another device, sign in with the same Google account. Still missing after a restart? Contact support with the order ID.

9. How do I cancel a subscription or ask for a refund?

iPhones: Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google in the store’s purchase history; the studio cannot issue store refunds. Cancelling a subscription does not remove lifetime purchases.

10. I want to delete my account and data

In the app: Settings → Delete Account (two confirmations). Web: /account-deletion/. This deletes the app account and cloud data. Store purchases and subscriptions stay with Apple/Google; cancel subscriptions separately. Deleting the account does not automatically refund purchases.

11. Daily reminders or streak notifications do not arrive

Enable notifications for Cube Pattern in the device settings, then check Settings → Notifications inside the app. Battery optimisation on some Android phones can block scheduled notifications; allow background activity for the app. Notifications are optional.

12. The game stutters, freezes or crashes

Close other apps and restart the device. Make sure the app is updated to the latest version. If it happens on a specific level, note the level number and patterns size and email support. Crash reports are optional and only sent with your consent.

13. There is no sound or music

Check the device’s mute switch and volume, then Settings → Audio inside the app. Music and SFX have separate toggles. If only one audio pack is silent, switch to the default pack.

14. Progress made offline did not sync

Open the app while online and keep it in the foreground for a few seconds; queued level results are sent automatically. Open Settings and check the sync status. If a level still does not appear, replay it online once — progress sync keeps the best stars.

15. I changed phones. How do I move my progress?

Install the app on the new phone and sign in with the same Google account. Cloud progress (levels, stars, coins, cosmetics) syncs automatically. Guest progress cannot move between devices.

16. The app is in the wrong language

Open Settings → Language and choose one of the six supported languages (English, Türkçe, Deutsch, Español, Français, Português). The game also follows the device language on first launch.
